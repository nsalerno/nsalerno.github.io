About

The purpose of this site is to be a reference for myself, accessible from any computer. I am a computer programmer. I don’t claim to be any kind of graphic artist or web designer. Thus, this site is more functional than fashionable. There are two components to this site, the blog and the notebook. The blog functions like a diary of thoughts. The notebook is a living document that I maintain, similar to a wiki (except only I edit the content).

Colors

The color scheme of this website is a reflection of me.

Indigo paired with gray, silver, beige, or white has always been my favorite color combination. Indigo is a hue that is darker than blue. It’s not the same as blue, which many people confuse the two. And yes, indigo is a bona fide color. If you use a prism to break up light, or simply look at a rainbow in the sky, you will see indigo as a first-class citizen. It is between blue and violet.

I think gray, silver, and white pair nicely with indigo. Which second color I choose depends on how I’m feeling. Gray lets me express when I feel uncertain as it represents lack of clarity. It’s like I kind-of know but I really don’t so there is more for me to learn. White lets me express when I do feel certain as it represents clarity and enlightenment. Silver simply looks nice to me. Beige allows me to show some color outside of the grayscale range. Beige is like a gray that is warm. That established, the white background used for content is in no way an indication that I write with total clarity and enlightenment. It's simply a contrast for the color of the text.

This color combination, coincidentally, also reveals something about me, according to Magic the Gathering (MTG). In Duncan Sabien’s excellent essay on MTG’s color wheel, blue represents “perfection through knowledge” and white represents “peace through order.” Well, that seems to align very much with my OCD tendencies. I feel like I hear it loud and clear. Do I achieve these goals? No, I don't and that's OK as long as I try my best, learn from trying, and have fun.

In Julio Torres’ Color Theories, which I enjoyed watching, he discusses the meaning of different colors. He describes navy blue, which as far as I’m concerned lands in the realm of indigo, as representing law and order. However, he does place a negative connotation on it as it tends to be bureaucracy that tries to hide an authoritative system behind the guise of a logically sound benevolent system. It’s an interesting concept to me because I do feel like we software engineers can be guilty of that when we proclaim that our new software framework will solve all your problems and make the world harmonious. These software frameworks have the navy blue vibe, as all we accomplish is trading one set of frustration with a different set of frustration. Julio also describes beige as “delightfully inept.” As someone who likes the color beige all I can say is ouch.

Regardless of the layout and color scheme, this is my website, my little corner of the internet, and I will have it may way that makes me happy.
