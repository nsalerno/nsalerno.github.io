Blog

Overview

This blog is a static journal of ideas, observations, and technical facts. Like any blog, each entry is written once and set in stone. I may append an updated note to an existing entry, however, if I have a change of opinion about an entry then that change will become a new blog posting. There is no particular order to posted entries. Thus, ideas will not follow any chronological or topological sense of order.

This blog is technically not a blog. I do not have a comments section. This website is new and I’m mainly trying to get its structure finalized. I will look into adding a commenting component at a later date. Apologies. In the meantime, if you see a page of interest feel free to submit it to a discussion forum.
