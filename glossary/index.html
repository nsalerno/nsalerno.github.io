Glossary

Overview

The glossary focuses on words we use in software engineering. These are not specialized words of the field. These are common words, which we misuse in inventive ways. This is my attempt to reign in the chaos and keep myself in check.

The glossary is designed to be a quick reference, as opposed to a proper formal dictionary. When I define a word, I am not trying to document all the definitions and usages of that word. I am only looking to record the one specific definition of the word that is most applicable in the context of software engineering.

Full disclosure: My definitions come from Merriam-Webster. I use their wording and phrasing. It is not of my own design. I will provide a link to Merriam-Webster’s definition but I will also copy a little of their text here. I would prefer to provide a link to an exact definition of a word but it appears (at the time of this writing) that Merriam-Webster does not provide HTML anchor links to specific definitions. Because I am not looking to copy over an abundance of words, and because I’m not interested in all the definitions of a particular word, I would hope that what I copy is considered fair use.

The format of each entry is the following:

  1. The word and a link to its source.
  2. An excerpt of the specific definition that I believe to be most applicable.
  3. Remarks of how I interpret the word and how I believe it should be used.

Well, dear reader, here’s to plain speaking and clear understanding.
