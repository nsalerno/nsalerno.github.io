Notebook

Overview

My notebook is a dynamic structure of living documents that reflect my understanding of any topic. It is documentation to help me to learn and to be a reference. The writing style will target an audience of someone who isn’t technical. This is done for two reasons. The first reason is so that anyone else can read this content and learn something. The second reason is to help me learn the material better. If I can’t explain it to another person like they are five years old then how well do I truly know the material? Thus, it keeps me honest in what I believe myself to understand.

This is not a blog. There is no posting on a date where that date serves as an identifier of the entry and where that posting is an immutable fact of the past. Instead, my writings are subject to revision (to correct errors and to improve understanding) and subject to reorganization. The goal is to create a story from a chronological beginning to the present, like a history book. I want the notebook to have the ability to be read in order starting with the basics and first principles, leading up to higher order concepts. As a consequence, bookmarks may be impacted. If you bookmark a particular URL then it may be subject to change. Sorry about that.
